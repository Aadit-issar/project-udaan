# UDAAN — 3-minute demo

Open https://aadit-issar.github.io/project-udaan/ (fresh / incognito window shows onboarding).

1. **The hook (15s)** — "Students don't fail because they don't care; they fail to *start*. UDAAN: drop the scroll, ace the goal."
2. **Explore demo (10s)** — click **Explore demo**. Point out the *Demo data* flag: sample data, real data untouched.
3. **Help Me Start (40s)** — click **Help Me Start** → choose *25 min*, energy *Low*. Show the recommended task (Chemistry, due tomorrow), the concrete **first step**, and the **"Recommended because…"** explanation. Click **Choose another task** once to show it's a suggestion, not a command.
4. **Task Quest (30s)** — click **Break into steps** → planner opens the quest. Tick a step (+8 XP), edit a step's text, reorder it.
5. **Focus Room (45s)** — open **Focus Room**. Orbit the 3D desk above the clouds, click the lamp. On the board panel press **Start**; the 2D timer card updates too — same state. In demo mode press **Demo: skip to the end** → "Were you mostly focused?" → **Yes, count it**.
6. **Progress World (20s)** — open **Skyworld**: XP added, milestone unlocked, island grows.
7. **VR (20s)** — on a Meta Quest browser: Focus Room → **Enter VR**, point controller at the panel and pull trigger to start/pause the timer. On a laptop, show the honest note: *"VR needs a WebXR headset…"*
8. **Close (10s)** — offline PWA, no account, no tracking. "Break the Delay, Build the Day."

**Backup plan:** if the 3D room is slow on the presentation machine, use **Switch to 2D** — every feature still works.
